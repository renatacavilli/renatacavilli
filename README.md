# Renata Cavilli

**AI & Quantitative Researcher at Alpha Options · Mathematics at Universitat de Barcelona**

I am a quantitative researcher and a final-year mathematics student. At Alpha Options I
research and build machine-learning models for financial time series. Most of that work is
research: forming a hypothesis, building the model from scratch, and trying to break the
result before I trust it.

The repositories here are personal projects, separate from that work. I use them to explore
the questions I find most interesting, with the same discipline: one clear question, real
public data, and code that anyone can reproduce.

## What I work on

- **Quantitative finance**: derivatives and volatility, systematic strategies, backtesting
  and the ways a backtest can mislead.
- **Stochastic processes**: stochastic differential equations, diffusions and regime changes
  in financial markets.
- **Time-series analysis and econometrics**: stationarity, cointegration, volatility models
  and spurious relationships between trending series.
- **Probability and statistics**: inference, resampling methods, hypothesis testing and
  multiple testing.
- **Machine learning and deep learning**: models for time series, ensembles, and validation
  that respects the order of time.
- **Numerical methods and optimisation**: Monte Carlo simulation, iterative solvers and
  numerical solution of differential equations.
- **Differential equations and dynamical systems**: stability, bifurcations and chaos.
- **Game theory**: strategic interaction and decision-making under uncertainty.
