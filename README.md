# Renata Cavilli

**AI & Quantitative Researcher at Alpha Options · Mathematics at Universitat de Barcelona**

I build and test machine-learning models for financial time series. Most of my work is
research: forming a hypothesis, building the model from scratch, and trying to break the
result before I trust it.

## What I work on

- **Machine learning for time series**: classification models and ensemble methods on
  high-frequency financial data.
- **Statistical validation**: walk-forward and blocked cross-validation, permutation and
  bootstrap tests, null baselines, and audits for look-ahead bias and overfitting.
- **Stochastic processes and market regimes**: my bachelor's thesis applies stochastic
  dynamical systems to regime changes in financial markets.
